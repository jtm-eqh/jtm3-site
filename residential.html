Residential Strategy

Acquisition, repositioning, and disposition of single-family assets across affordable and workforce housing segments in the Southeast.

Strategy 01

Single-Family Residential

JTM3 Properties invests in workforce housing and entry-level residential assets in high-growth Southeast markets. Our strategy centers on acquiring homes at a discounted or structured basis, providing alternative financing solutions to underserved buyers, and generating income through long-term cash flow and financing spreads.

We focus on assets that are highly liquid, financeable, and resilient across market cycles — targeting communities where homeownership demand outpaces conventional financing availability.

Strategy Details

How We Invest in Residential

Acquisition Approach

Acquiring homes at discounted or structured basis through off-market channels, wholesale networks, and motivated seller pipelines in Southeast markets.

Owner Financing

Providing alternative financing solutions to underserved buyers — including ITIN holders, self-employed, and credit-challenged buyers — who cannot access conventional loans.

Income Generation

Revenue through financing spreads, monthly cash flow, and interest income on owner-financed positions with embedded equity cushions and collateral protection.

Target Markets

High-growth Southeast metros with constrained housing supply, strong population inflows, and demographic tailwinds supporting workforce housing demand.

Asset Profile

Workforce and affordable housing — highly liquid, financeable assets resilient across market cycles with strong end-buyer demand and multiple exit paths.

Exit Optionality

Retail sale, note sale, refinance, or long-term hold — multiple exits underwritten and stress-tested at acquisition to maximize flexibility across market conditions.
