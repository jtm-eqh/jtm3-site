Portfolio Spotlight

Equidad Homes — Alternative Homeownership

Equidad Homes is an alternative homeownership platform operating across 12 Texas markets. The company provides owner-financed residential sales to buyers who cannot access conventional financing — including ITIN holders, self-employed individuals, and credit-challenged borrowers.

Equidad Homes operates a two-program model — Open Market acquisitions and Sub-To/Wrap acquisitions — with EQH Financial (NMLS #2832795) serving as the licensed mortgage entity for all RMLO and closing functions.

What We Are Actively Pursuing

JTM3 Properties continuously evaluates opportunities across all three investment strategies. We are particularly active in the following areas.

Single-Family Acquisitions

Off-market and wholesale single-family homes in Southeast growth markets — acquired at basis for owner-financed resale or rental income generation.

Land & Lot Purchases

Raw and semi-entitled residential land in Texas and Southeast path-of-growth markets — targeting parcels with clear development or lot-sale exit paths.
