Common Questions

Land Seller FAQ

What states do you buy land in?
We primarily buy land across the Southeast — Texas, Louisiana, Mississippi, Alabama, Georgia, Florida, Tennessee, and surrounding states. If you're outside this area, contact us and we'll let you know if we can help.
Do you buy land with back taxes or liens?
Yes. We frequently purchase land with delinquent taxes, liens, or title issues. We work with title companies to resolve these at closing — you don't have to fix anything before selling.
How do you determine the offer price?
We research county records, recent comparable land sales, zoning, road access, utilities, and market conditions. Our offers reflect what we can pay cash for a fast, as-is purchase — at a discount to retail in exchange for speed, certainty, and simplicity.
Is there any obligation when I submit?
None. Submitting your information lets us research and prepare an offer. You are under no obligation to accept and there is no cost to you at any point in the process.
Can you buy if I only know the approximate location?
Yes — start with county, state, and approximate acreage. Once you're interested in our offer we'll identify the parcel together and handle the rest from there.
