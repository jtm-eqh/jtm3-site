05Questions

Common questions from landowners.

Where do you buy land?

We buy across Texas and the Southeast, including Louisiana, Mississippi, Alabama, Georgia, Florida and Tennessee. If your land is elsewhere, contact us and we will let you know if we can help.

Can you buy land with back taxes or liens?

Often, yes. Delinquent taxes and liens can usually be paid off from the sale at closing, so you do not need to fix them first.

How do you determine the price?

We review county records, recent land sales nearby, zoning, access, utilities and market conditions. Our offer reflects a fast, certain cash purchase with no commissions or costs on your side.

Do I need a survey or to visit the property?

No. You do not need a survey to receive an offer, and closings can be completed remotely.

Am I obligated if I request an offer?

No. Requesting an offer is free and does not commit you to anything.
