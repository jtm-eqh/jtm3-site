What We Purchase

Six Note Types We Buy for Cash

Active buyers of residential mortgage notes at any stage of the loan lifecycle.

Performing Notes

Current, paying 1st and 2nd lien positions. We provide liquidity so you can redeploy capital without waiting for the note to mature.

Non-Performing Notes

Notes 30+ days late, in default, or in foreclosure. We specialize in NPLs in non-judicial Southeast states where we have established workout expertise.

Seller-Finance Notes

Owner-financed notes created at property sale — residential, land, or commercial. We buy the note and give you cash now.

Note Portfolios

Multiple notes from a single seller or institution. Streamlined due diligence and a single closing — we move fast on tape acquisitions.

Partial Purchases

Not ready to sell the full note? We can purchase a defined number of future payments while you retain the back-end of the loan.

Estate & Inherited Notes

One closing, one check. No ongoing collections or servicing obligations for heirs or estate executors.

Who We Work With

Six Types of Note Sellers

Private Note Holders

Individuals who financed the sale of their own property and want to convert monthly income into a lump-sum cash payment.

02
