05Questions

Common questions from note holders.

Why is the offer less than the balance?

A note pays out over many years, and a buyer takes on the risk that payments stop. The price reflects the time value of money and that risk. Better payment history, a lower balance relative to the property’s value and a first-lien position all raise the offer.

Do you buy non-performing notes?

Yes. We buy notes that are late, in default or in foreclosure, and we take on the work of resolving them.

What documents do you need?

The promissory note, the mortgage or deed of trust, and a payment history. A closing statement from the original sale and any title policy help us quote faster.

Can I sell only part of my note?

Yes. With a partial purchase we buy a set number of upcoming payments and you keep the rest of the note.

How accurate is the online estimate?

It is an illustrative range based on the details you enter. Your firm quote depends on our review of the documents, payment history, title and property.

Are there fees to sell?

There are no fees to request an estimate or quote, and no obligation to accept.
