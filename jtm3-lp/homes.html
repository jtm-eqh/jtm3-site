05Questions

Common questions from homeowners.

How do you decide on the offer?

We look at the condition of the house, the cost of repairs and recent sales of similar homes nearby. Our offer reflects a fast, as-is cash purchase with no commissions or repairs on your side, and we explain the numbers when we send it.

Are there any fees or commissions?

No. You pay no commissions and no fees to work with us, and we pay standard closing costs.

Do I need to make repairs or clean out the house?

No. Sell it as it is. Leave behind anything you do not want and we will take care of it.

How fast can you close?

In as few as 14 days, once title is clear. If you need more time, you choose a later date.

What if I am behind on payments or facing foreclosure?

Contact us as soon as possible. Selling before a foreclosure is completed may help you keep equity and protect your credit, and the earlier we talk, the more options you have.

Am I obligated if I request an offer?

No. Requesting an offer is free and does not commit you to anything.
