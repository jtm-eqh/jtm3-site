Strategy 03

Credit & Mortgage Notes

JTM3 operates as a credit-focused investor, transforming real estate into yield-generating financial instruments. Our strategy includes originating and acquiring residential notes and structured finance positions, capturing interest rate spreads and fee-based income, and investing in asset-backed, amortizing cash flow streams.

This approach allows us to generate consistent yield while maintaining asset-level collateral protection — targeting performing and non-performing residential mortgage notes in non-judicial foreclosure states with favorable workout and recovery dynamics.

Strategy Details

How We Invest in Notes

Note Origination

Self-originated seller-finance notes on residential acquisitions, capturing spread between cost of capital and borrower rate with embedded equity cushion.

Note Acquisition

Acquiring performing and non-performing residential mortgage notes in non-judicial foreclosure states with favorable workout and recovery dynamics.

Yield Profile

Asset-backed, amortizing income streams with embedded collateral protection. Rate arbitrage captured between borrowing cost and note yield.

Portfolio Construction

Diversified across geographies, loan-to-values, and borrower profiles to manage concentration risk and smooth overall cash flow to investors.

Non-Judicial States

Focus on Southeast states with favorable non-judicial foreclosure processes, providing efficient enforcement rights and collateral recovery when needed.

Collateral Protection

Every note position is backed by real property collateral — providing asset-level downside protection and multiple resolution paths for each position.
