Land Strategy

Acquiring land and lots, entitling for development or resale — either traditionally or through owner-financed notes across path-of-growth Southeast markets.

Strategy 02

Land & Development

JTM3 targets entitled and pre-development land opportunities positioned for future residential and mixed-use growth. Our strategy includes investing in large-scale master-planned communities, navigating entitlements, infrastructure, and utility structuring, and creating value through land positioning and phased development execution.

We focus on path-of-growth markets where long-term demand supports absorption and value creation — acquiring raw, entitled, and semi-improved land with clear paths to residential absorption and builder demand.

Strategy Details

How We Invest in Land

Land Sourcing

Raw, entitled, and semi-improved land in high-growth Southeast corridors with clear residential absorption paths and near-term builder demand.

Value Creation

Entitlements, platting, infrastructure improvements, and utility extensions to unlock development potential and increase per-unit land economics.

Master-Planned Communities

Investing in large-scale community developments with phased execution, delivering both land appreciation and ongoing cash flow through lot sales.

Owner-Financed Notes

Capturing spread and retaining asset exposure through owner-financed lot and land note creation — generating yield while maintaining collateral protection.

Traditional Disposition

Bulk or lot-by-lot sales to homebuilders and developers — positioning JTM3 as a reliable land supplier in high-growth Southeast markets.

Target Geography

Path-of-growth Southeast markets where population inflows, infrastructure investment, and housing demand support long-term land absorption and value creation.
