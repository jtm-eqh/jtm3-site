01The Firm

A multi-strategy real estate investor built around underwriting, structure, and control of the full lifecycle.

JTM3 Properties provides customized investment opportunities across single-family homes, residential land, and mortgage notes. The firm sponsors and co-sponsors investments across these asset categories and advises clients through each stage of an investment.

Our edge is structure. We acquire at a discounted or structured basis, create or assume underlying debt where terms favor investors, and use owner financing to reach buyers that conventional lenders do not serve. Every position is underwritten with more than one exit and backed by real property collateral.

02Investment Strategies

Three complementary strategies on one platform.

Each strategy stands alone and feeds the others. Residential acquisitions create notes, land is sold on owner-financed terms, and the note book generates the cash flow that funds the next acquisition.

Strategy I

Single-Family Residential

Acquisition, repositioning, and disposition of workforce and entry-level homes in high-growth markets where homeownership demand outpaces conventional financing.

Alternative financing for ITIN holders, self-employed, and credit-challenged buyers who cannot access a conventional loan.

Income

Financing spreads, monthly cash flow, and interest income on owner-financed positions with an embedded equity cushion.

Multiple Exits

Each deal is underwritten with at least two exits and stress-tested before we commit capital.
